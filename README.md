# Arduino-Curriculum-Advanced

**Course Overview:** Learn how to build and program your own electronics. This course starts with basic electricity and ends with building moving robots.

---

## Unit 1: Electronics Basics
*Before coding, you must understand how electricity flows.*

* **1.1 Electricity Fundamentals**
    * Difference between Static and Current electricity.
    * The Big Three: Voltage ($V$), Current ($I$), and Resistance ($R$).
* **1.2 Essential Laws**
    * **Ohm’s Law:** The math used to keep components from burning out.
    * **Series vs. Parallel:** Different ways to connect parts in a circuit.
* **1.3 Identifying Parts**
    * **Resistors:** How to read the colored stripes to find their value.
    * **Tolerance:** Understanding that parts aren't always 100% perfect.

## Unit 2: The Arduino Board
*Getting to know the "brain" of your projects.*

* **2.1 Hardware Tour**
    * Identifying the pins and ports on an Arduino Uno.
    * **Microcontrollers:** Why we use them instead of a full computer.
* **2.2 Building Without Solder**
    * **The Breadboard:** How to use the "plugin" board to build circuits fast.
    * **Simulators:** How to test your ideas on a computer before buying parts.



---

## Unit 3: Writing the Code
*Learning the language used to talk to the hardware.*

* **3.1 The Arduino Software (IDE)**
    * How to write, check, and send code to your board.
* **3.2 Inputs and Outputs**
    * **Digital:** Simple ON/OFF signals (like a light switch).
    * **Analog:** Signals that vary (like a volume knob or a dimmer switch).
* **3.3 Using Libraries**
    * How to download "shortcut" code to make complex parts work instantly.

---

## Unit 4: Sensors and Motion
*Giving your project "senses" and the ability to move.*

* **4.1 Screens and Lights**
    * Using 7-Segment displays to show numbers and timers.
* **4.2 Senses**
    * **Ultrasonic Sensors:** Using sound to measure distance.
    * **IR Sensors:** Using light to detect objects or follow lines.
* **4.3 Motors**
    * **DC Motors:** Controlling speed for wheels.
    * **Servo Motors:** Moving an arm or a flap to a specific angle.

## Unit 5: Building Robots
*Putting everything together into a final project.*

* **5.1 Autonomous Robots**
    * **Line Followers:** Robots that follow a path on the floor.
    * **Obstacle Avoidance:** Robots that drive around without hitting walls.
* **5.2 Advanced Projects**
    * **Remote Control:** Driving your car with a controller.
    * **Complex Motion:** Introduction to "Hexapods" (six-legged walking robots).
